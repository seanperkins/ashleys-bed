Use the guide in the shop

  1. Start with Planning. Confirm the installation site before committing to purchases or cabinet fabrication.
  2. Keep a personal checklist. Checks and worksheet notes save in this browser only. Export them to share with the operator; they are not a machining approval.
  3. Print the page for the task. Use “Print this page” for the purchasing list, setup worksheet or assembly sequence.
  4. Use the matching revision. Download files from this guide together. Old two-cabinet drawings, quantities and budgets are not interchangeable with this revision.
Important installation boundary

The three bed wall brackets are hidden in the CAD view, not optional in construction. Retain both headboards and all manufacturer-required anchoring into at least three suitable wooden studs. A qualified installer and an adult helper are required; the left bookcase needs its own anti-tip restraint.

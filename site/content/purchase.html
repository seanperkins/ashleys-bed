What to ask the plywood supplier

  1. Require cabinet-grade, all-veneer-core stock. No MDF or particleboard core; confirm the complete core construction, not just the face species or a brand name. Ask for flat panels, sound face veneers and minimal core voids, particularly at hardware and joinery locations.
  2. Obtain actual thickness measurements before accepting the stock. Nominal 3/4 inch, 18 mm and 23/32 inch are not interchangeable dimensions. Measure the proposed 1/2- and 1/4-inch material too. Approve any necessary design changes against the Rockler requirements before a production order or cut.
  3. Preserve the grain direction. Every part’s Length_in follows the stock’s long grain direction. Confirm grain along the 96-inch full-sheet length and the 48-inch project-panel length. Do not rotate parts merely to force a smaller nest.
  4. Quote the exact sizes and handling. Ask whether the 24 × 48-inch drawer panel is available with the required core and grain, whether dimensions are truly usable, and what delivery or rough breakdown costs. No automatic full-sheet fallback is included. Avoid supplier cuts until the reviewed layout and safe transport sizes agree.
  5. Recheck the count after fabrication decisions. The nominal layout uses 3/4-inch edge margins and 1/2-inch gaps. Final joints, real sheet sizes, defects, holding strategy and test coupons may change stock needs. Decide any spare material explicitly; this is not a minimum-sheet optimization claim.

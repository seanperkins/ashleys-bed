Use this as a build-stage checklist beside the complete Rockler instructions. Do not start fabrication until the planning decisions and machining inputs are resolved.

This is not a substitute for the manufacturer’s installation procedure. Read the entire manual before work. A qualified person must attach the bed to the wall, and another adult must help during installation. The mechanism can close suddenly without a mattress. Do not work alone, omit either headboard, substitute drywall anchors, or use an impact driver. Rockler safety warnings, p. 3.

Prepare the parts

Stage 1 — Identify the kit and inventory everything

  1. Confirm the actual kit is the horizontal queen version. Compare the supplied instructions with Rockler’s latest product instructions before using this February 2025 reference.
  2. Lay out and identify the frame members, mounting plates, threaded inserts, pistons, legs, door brackets, mattress supports, 40 queen slats and all mounting hardware. Use the purchase guide to separate kit contents from additional purchases.
  3. Resolve the manual’s conflicts with Rockler before substitutions: BB is listed as M8 × 45 mm on p. 5 but M8 × 50 mm on p. 13; W screw quantities differ between inventory and assembly text; four M6 nuts OO appear on p. 16 but not the inventory. Do not infer replacement fasteners.
  4. Check tools, PPE, supported work surfaces, clamps, a square, a level, a suitable doweling jig, depth stops and safe access equipment. Follow each tool’s instructions and each coating’s safety data sheet.

Sources: Rockler pp. 2–3, safety; pp. 4–7, inventory, cabinet parts and tools; p. 13 and pp. 16–18, conflicting fastener references.

Stage 2 — Check stock, machine approved details and dry-fit

  1. Measure actual plywood thickness and reject unsuitable or damaged stock. The bed cabinet requires suitable plywood, not MDF, particleboard or melamine-coated versions of those materials. Confirm the required interior opening against the actual kit.
  2. Label all 27 parts with their sheet/part ID, assembly, inside face, front edge and grain direction. Follow the part-to-sheet map; shared sheets are not separate purchases for each assembly.
  3. Use only the finalized machining plan. The available rectangular DXFs omit joinery, drilling and cutter compensation. A flat-sheet CNC setup does not automatically produce every edge-bored dowel or screw hole: an approved jig or separate documented setup may do that work.
  4. Dry-fit the panels and inspect square, face orientation and joint seating before glue. Keep the bed’s Rockler dowel-and-screw construction unchanged; do not apply the proposed bookcase dados to the bed carcass.
  5. Mill the allocated S02 stop blank to ⅝ × 3 × 56 in finished. Its final contact location is fitted later to the actual installed frame, not copied from the representative model.

Sources: Rockler p. 6, cabinet parts and opening; pp. 8–10, drilling and construction. Stock labels and CNC release checks are project workflow, not additional Rockler instructions.

Build the two cabinets separately

Stage 3 — Mount the plates and assemble the bed carcass

  1. Follow the left/right drilling guide on p. 8 for the 16 threaded inserts and mounting plates. Confirm handedness and front-edge references before drilling. Use the specified inserts and M8 screws; ordinary wood screws are not an acceptable plate attachment.
  2. Assemble the top/upper-headboard and bottom/lower-headboard pairs using Rockler’s dowel locations, hole depths, glue and screw procedure on p. 9. Together these joints require six dowels and eight #8 × 2 in screws.
  3. Attach both side panels to those assemblies using the reinforced joints on p. 10: sixteen more dowels and twenty-four more #8 × 2 in screws. Clamp and check square as the joints come together; remove glue squeeze-out.
  4. Verify the complete opening against the manufacturer’s requirement before continuing. Keep both headboards intact. Do not overtighten screws or conceal a damaged joint with filler.

Bed-carcass minimum: 22 hardwood dowels (⅜ × 1½ in) and 32 #8 × 2 in wood screws, plus wood glue. Purchase-pack leftovers are not a specified left-cabinet fastener schedule. Sources: Rockler p. 8 and pp. 9–10.

Stage 4 — Build the left cabinet only after its joinery is approved

Fabrication hold: the custom left cabinet is not covered by the Rockler manual. Shallow dados/rabbets for fixed parts are proposed, but their widths/depths, resulting part lengths, back attachment, drawer-bottom support and fastener layout are not finalized. Do not cut those features from the nominal model.

  1. The owner and fabricator must approve fixed top/bottom/shelf joinery and the back/plinth details using measured stock. Dry-fit the carcass square and verify supported fastening locations before glue-up.
  2. Keep the three upper shelves adjustable. Choose the actual 5 mm pins, define their bore diameter/depth/edge distances and set shelf fitting clearance. The three shelves need 12 supports; their nominal 28.5 in width is not a finished fitting allowance.
  3. Select the exact 14 in slide pair before sizing or drilling the drawer. Verify its required side clearance, mounting pattern and compatible screws; the modeled ½ in-per-side clearance is only nominal. Confirm drawer joints, bottom support and false-front screw penetration against the real stack.
  4. Fit the drawer, false front and pull after the cabinet is square. Keep glue off adjustable parts and prevent screws from protruding through thin drawer walls.

Project basis: current nominal cabinet cut list and hardware requirements. The selected slide, shelf-pin and restraint manufacturers’ instructions must supply the missing hardware-specific details; no model has yet been selected to cite.

Stage 5 — Prepare and finish the wood

  1. Trial-fit hardware first. Fill cosmetic defects and sand without cutting through face veneers. Follow the chosen finish system’s preparation and dust-removal instructions; filler does not repair a structural joint.
  2. Mask bare glue surfaces wherever finishing precedes assembly. Protect mounting interfaces and moving hardware from coating buildup; do not paint slide bearing surfaces.
  3. Approve a dried sample of the design navy, #182B49. Apply the selected compatible primer and cabinet enamel according to their labels, including drying, sanding, recoating and full-cure requirements.
  4. Plan one primer coat and two finish coats over about 289.3 sq ft of painted faces and edges. At the planning coverage of 350 sq ft/gallon, this allows one gallon primer and two gallons enamel. Actual coverage, edge sealing and extra coats can increase use.
  5. Leave the drawer-box walls and bottom natural or use the optional compatible clear finish. These five pieces are excluded from the navy-paint area. Allow finishes and glue to cure before loading and hardware adjustment.

Finish quantities are project allowances, not Rockler specifications. Follow the selected product label and SDS; Rockler p. 2 requires chemical/tool safety practices, and p. 10 discusses concealing cabinet screw holes.

Anchor before installing the loaded mechanism

Stage 6 — Install all headboard anchors and all three wall brackets

  1. Have the qualified installer and adult helper move the bed cabinet into position. Resolve base molding, level and plumb it, and establish flush contact as required by pp. 11–12.
  2. Locate at least three 2 × 4 or larger wooden studs. Install six of the supplied ¼ × 3 in lag bolts through the upper/lower headboards into those studs using the manufacturer’s layout and pilot-hole procedure.
  3. Install all three top wall brackets HH at actual stud locations, flush to the wall and cabinet top. Follow every dowel, through-bolt and screw step on p. 12: the remaining six lag bolts anchor the brackets to the studs; three through-bolts II and six washer-head screws JJ secure the bracket/top connections.
  4. Inspect the complete anchoring system: 12 lag bolts total, both headboards, three top brackets. The model hides the brackets only for presentation. Do not omit hardware or replace stud fastening with drywall anchors.
  5. Install the left cabinet’s separate rated restraint using its manufacturer’s instructions and suitable structure. Do not rely only on the ¼ in back. Optional cabinet connector bolts align the cabinets; they do not restrain either cabinet against tipping.

Stop if the wall or flush-contact requirements cannot be met. The installer must resolve the condition with Rockler, not improvise an anchor substitution. Do not unscrew the bed cabinet from the wall once pistons are installed. Sources: Rockler pp. 11–12; p. 14.

Follow the manufacturer’s mechanism sequence

Keep the actual manual open at the named pages. The stages below identify order and inspection gates, not replacement instructions for piston mounting, pivot retention or frame handling.

Stage 7 — Assemble the steel frame and initial slats

Follow Rockler p. 13 for frame orientation, support tabs and the exploded assembly. Resolve BB fastener length with Rockler first. Leave fasteners loose until the frame is assembled as directed, then tighten them. Install slats and caps per p. 14, deliberately leaving the first and last rows out until the later step.

Stage 8 — Fit pistons, retained pivots, temporary stops and legs

With the cabinet fully anchored and the adult helper present, follow pp. 14–16 in their exact order: frame-side piston attachment; seating pivots in mounting-plate saddles; saddle locking plates and their fasteners; controlled frame positioning; cabinet-side piston attachment; temporary stops; then legs and foot bar. Follow the specified washers, locking nuts and recommended removable threadlocker at the saddle-lock fasteners. Resolve the OO nut inventory discrepancy before the foot-bar step.

Once the pistons are attached, the mechanism is under pressure. Do not release a pulled-down frame: it can snap upward. The bed will not stay down without a mattress. An adult helper must control it as instructed; do not stand on it without an adult holding it down. Do not detach the cabinet from the wall with pistons installed.

Stage 9 — Fit and secure the four door panels

Use the queen bracket pattern and orientation in pp. 16–17. Choose the actual pulls before drilling their fixing holes. Hang and adjust the four panels, then perform the manufacturer’s clearance check before permanent attachment: their outside edges must not contact the mounting plates or hardware, including when the legs are retracted and the bed is lowered beyond normal horizontal during that supervised check. Follow p. 18 for final attachment and its prohibition on driving screws through the center frame support. Do not resolve the W screw-count discrepancy by guessing.

Stage 10 — Fit the permanent stop and remaining supports

Follow pp. 18–19, Final Touches. Fit the ⅝ in-thick permanent stop centered and set back so the actual frame closes plumb. Secure it with at least four #8 × 1¼ in wood screws, not supplied in the kit. Install the mattress supports and the remaining first/last slat rows as directed. Maintain adult control of the unloaded frame throughout.

Commission before normal use

Use Rockler p. 20 for lowering the frame, deploying legs, loading the mattress with an adult helper and carefully closing it. The mattress must be 60 × 80 in, at most 10 in thick and 132 lb; foam or very light mattresses are prohibited. Confirm the full moving assembly—door panels, hardware, mattress and bedding—against p. 3: maximum dynamic load 395 lb; suggested dynamic weight 280–395 lb. The 132 lb mattress limit is not a target or minimum. Mattress compatibility is retained without adding a mattress purchase or cost.

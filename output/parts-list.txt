FINISH AREA AND PACKAGE ALLOWANCES
Existing area method retained: both faces plus all four edges of every painted part,
using finished thickness. Per-part square feet = [2 x width x length +
2 x thickness x (width + length)] / 144, multiplied by quantity (dimensions in inches).
The four natural-finish drawer-box walls and its bottom are excluded from navy paint.
Main: 179.0994 sq ft faces + 9.8533 sq ft edges = 188.9527 sq ft.
LEFT: 94.7492 sq ft faces + 5.4670 sq ft edges = 100.2162 sq ft.
Total: 289.1689 sq ft (22 painted parts). Rounded total: about 289.2 sq ft.
Primer: 289.1689 / 350 x one coat = 0.8262 gallon; purchase allowance ONE gallon.
Enamel: 289.1689 / 350 x two coats = 1.6524 gallons; purchase allowance TWO gallons.
Actual product coverage, edge sealing, samples and extra coats may require more.
Keep glue surfaces bare; these geometric areas are an allowance, not precise consumption.

MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS VS PURCHASE PACKAGES
One slide pair, three pulls and one separate cabinet restraint kit are required.
Three adjustable shelves require 12 pins; allowance is one 20-pin pack, leaving 8 spares.
Bed-only requirements stay 32 #8 x 2-inch screws, 22 dowels and at least 4 stop screws.
Larger screw/dowel packages, glue, shims and finish supplies are purchased once; retained
pack allowances are NOT a final cabinet fastener count and are not blindly halved.
Four cabinet connector sets are optional alignment hardware, never wall restraints.
No supplier price, stock confirmation or new project-cost claim is included.

INSTALLATION AND SAFETY
Use every required bed anchor and all three top wall brackets even though the model hides them.
The queen bed must attach to at least three 2x4-or-larger wooden studs. No drywall anchors,
metal-stud substitution, masonry installation or impact driver. Installation requires a
qualified person and an adult helper. The 1/4-inch LEFT cabinet back is not an anti-tip anchor.
Do not eliminate either bed headboard. Do not use MDF/particleboard for the bed cabinet.
Mattress must be 60 x 80, at most 10 inches thick and 132 lb, and not foam or very light.
Check the full moving-assembly weight against manual p3; hardware motion in CAD is illustrative.
